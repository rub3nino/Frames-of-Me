# Brag Plan: Frames of Me

## What is this app?
Frames of Me (framesofme.com) finds your event photos from a single selfie: photographers upload all the shots, a self-hosted face-recognition engine indexes them, and each attendee sees **only** the photos they appear in — then the selfie is deleted. Built for a conference scale of ~150,000 photos and ~6,000 people.

## The angle
Two worlds of one event album, shown side by side:
1. **The common gallery** — everyone at the event uploads into one shared album.
2. **The private-recognition folder** — photographers upload the official shots, and your face is the key that pulls out exactly, and only, yours.
The video's quiet magic is the recognition beat: a wall of thousands of strangers' photos collapses into *your* gallery. Earnest, premium, privacy-first — no jokes. It should feel like an Apple product film, because the real UI already is one (Apple-grade, light-only design system).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dense wall of event photos tiles in fast — hundreds of faces, overwhelming. A small pill reads "Biruitori 2026 · 4.000+ scatti". Then one serif line slams and holds: **"Dove sei, tra tutte queste foto?"** The problem, stated calmly. That question earns the next 20 seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **La galleria comune** — photos streaming in from many people into one shared event album; a row of small upload chips/avatars ("tutti caricano"). One line: "Tutti caricano. Un unico album."
- **Il selfie** — recreate the real Selfie screen: a rounded camera oval with a face centered, the challenge hint "Tieni il viso nell'ovale". A soft shutter. Line: "Scatta un selfie."
- **Il riconoscimento** — the centerpiece. From the wall of thousands, the matching shots light up and assemble into the personal gallery "Le tue foto"; thumbnails pop in one by one. Line: "Solo le foto in cui compari."
- **Privacy** — shield, calm: "Il tuo viso resta tuo. Il selfie si cancella dopo la ricerca." EU-hosted, self-hosted.

## Outro / punchline
The bracket-and-dot camera-frame mark draws in; the wordmark **Frames of Me** settles with the periwinkle dot. Tagline: "Le tue foto dell'evento, trovate in un selfie." Then `framesofme.com`. Soft fade.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, from the real participant app:
1. The shared event wall / common gallery (entry context — everyone's photos).
2. **Scatta un selfie** — the camera-oval screen with the challenge (key action).
3. **Le tue foto** — recognition assembles your personal gallery, only the frames you're in (result).
This is the centerpiece; the wall and the privacy band frame it.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with app-store feature-card clarity for the two-worlds beats)
- Creative direction: "quiet premium product film — your face is the key to your photos"
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer settled holds, soft crossfades/slides, light-to-medium type, nothing aggressive; confidence through restraint. The recognition moment is the single authored flourish.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, confident product-film bed with sparse, motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM, calm business-warm mood)
- Music treatment: start at 0, bed at a restrained level, gentle fade-in over ~0.4s, soft fade-out over the last ~1s of the outro; lean on strong cues for scene changes, not a loud beat-match
- Music cue guidance: see section below
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the recognition-moment glow and the personal-gallery assembly may breathe gently with the bass; no waveform bars
- SFX posture: sparse, professional restraint — one soft camera shutter on the selfie, light ticks as matched thumbnails pop in, a single soft confirm tone on the recognition reveal, a quiet logo settle on the outro
- Audio-coupled moments: selfie shutter; sequential thumbnail pops in the personal gallery; the recognition reveal swell
- Restraint rule: audio must never get loud or playful; no risers, no whooshes stacked on every cut, nothing that undercuts the privacy/premium feel

## Music cue guidance
- Track: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, ~114.84 BPM (bundled preset).
- Strong cues to target for major moments: **4.23s** (hook line settles / first reveal), **6.34s** (into the common gallery), **10.54s** (selfie → recognition), **12.65s** (personal gallery assembled).
- Beat-grid windows for sequential thumbnail pops: snap every other beat (~1.05s apart) starting ~11.6s so each thumbnail is a readable arrival, then hold the full set.
- Restraint note: polished tone — favor the strong cues and keep accents quiet; don't fill every beat.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The wall / hook — 3.5s
A dense grid of event photos tiles in fast (soft-blur-up) and fills the canvas — the overwhelming "all the shots" feeling. A small pill top-left: "Biruitori 2026 · 4.000+ scatti". The serif hook line slams in and holds centered on a soft scrim: **"Dove sei, tra tutte queste foto?"**
Sequential/interaction: yes — photo tiles flood in quickly (grid fill), then the line lands over them.
Audio intent: a gentle swell building curiosity; bed fades in.
Audio-coupled idea: land the hook line on the 4.23s strong cue.
Music: warm, low, rising.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — La galleria comune — 4s
Shift to a clean shared-album view: a tidy masonry of photos with a header "La galleria comune". A short row of small upload chips/avatars arrives ("tutti caricano"), each adding a photo into the one album. One line settles: **"Tutti caricano. Un unico album."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3-4 upload chips arrive one by one, each dropping a photo into the shared grid.
Audio intent: light, communal, moving forward; land the section on the 6.34s cue.
Audio-coupled idea: a very soft tick as each photo joins the album.
Music: steady, warm.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Il selfie — 4.5s
Recreate the real participant Selfie screen on a white card: a rounded camera-oval with a centered face silhouette/photo, the hint "Tieni il viso nell'ovale, a circa 40 cm", and the primary button "Scatta un selfie". A soft shutter fires; the oval pulses once. Small privacy note under it: "Lo usiamo solo per trovarti."
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate the shutter firing (button press → oval flash).
Audio intent: a single, clean, deliberate shutter; this is the pivot. Land the shutter near the 10.54s cue.
Audio-coupled idea: camera shutter SFX on the press; soft confirm tone as it captures.
Music: holds, slight lift under the shutter.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Il riconoscimento → Le tue foto — 4.5s
The centerpiece. The dense wall from Scene 1 returns briefly; the matching shots light up (soft accent-blue ring) while the rest dim and fall away, and the matches fly/assemble into a clean personal gallery titled "Le tue foto". Thumbnails pop in one by one. One line holds: **"Solo le foto in cui compari."** A small count ticks (e.g. "24 foto trovate").
Sequential/interaction: yes — matches highlight, then 4-5 thumbnails pop into the gallery one by one (~every other beat from ~11.6s), count ticks up; hold the full gallery.
Audio intent: the quiet magic — a soft confirm swell on the assemble (12.65s cue), gentle ticks per thumbnail.
Audio-coupled idea: light ticks on each thumbnail pop; subtle bass-breath glow on the match rings.
Music: confident, resolving.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Privacy — 3.5s
Calm, lots of space. The shield mark draws in. Line: **"Il tuo viso resta tuo."** Sub: "Il selfie si cancella dopo la ricerca. Hosting in Europa." Nothing else moves.
Sequential/interaction: none — one settled statement.
Audio intent: music eases back, breathing room; trust.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: softened bed.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Logo outro — 2.5s
The bracket-and-dot camera-frame mark draws, then the wordmark **Frames of Me** settles with the periwinkle dot. Tagline below: "Le tue foto dell'evento, trovate in un selfie." Then `framesofme.com`. Soft fade to canvas.
Sequential/interaction: yes — mark draws, wordmark + tagline + URL settle in sequence.
Audio intent: a quiet logo settle; bed fades out over the last ~1s.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft logo-settle tick.
Music: resolve and fade.
Transition mood: fade out (end)
